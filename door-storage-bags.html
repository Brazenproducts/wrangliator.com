What Are Wrangliator Bags?

Wrangliator bags are interior door storage organizers made by Bartact specifically for the Jeep Wrangler JL/JLU and Jeep Gladiator JT. They're designed to fit the exact door pocket shape of the 2018+ Wrangler and 2019+ Gladiator platform — not a universal bag forced into a space it wasn't made for.

The JL/JLU and Gladiator JT share a similar door pocket architecture, but the bags are cut to the specific dimensions of each platform. If you've been shoving stuff into the stock door pockets and watching it rattle around, Wrangliator bags are the fix.

Why Bartact Builds These

Bartact has been making Jeep-specific accessories in Temecula, California since 2004. The Wrangliator bags came out of a simple problem: the aftermarket had seat covers and grab handles covered, but interior door storage was still an afterthought. Most bags on the market are generic pouches with MOLLE on the back — they don't actually fit the JL/JLU or Gladiator door pocket geometry.

Bartact designed the Wrangliator bags to slot into the existing door pocket, use the space efficiently, and hold up to the same punishment as their seat covers. No universal sizing, no compromises.

What Fits in a Wrangliator Bag?

The bags are designed for real trail use. Depending on configuration, they fit:

  • Full-size water bottles (up to 32oz)
  • Recovery gloves and small hand tools
  • Phone, radio, or GPS unit
  • Maps, registration, and trail documents
  • Snacks, first aid basics, and EDC gear
